Get a second opinion on the current diff. Focus: $ARGUMENTS

1. Review it yourself first and note your findings — you need something to
   compare against.
2. Invoke the `codex-reviewer` subagent.
3. Present the findings in three buckets:
   - **Both of us flagged it** — highest confidence, fix these
   - **Only Codex flagged it** — the point of the exercise; evaluate each
   - **Only I flagged it** — say why you think Codex missed it
4. Recommend an action per blocking finding. Do not fix anything yet.
